PROJECT: Snowy Gothic Mountain Castle

TARGET:
approximately 130 blocks wide
95 blocks deep
135 blocks tall

Create an enormous gothic fantasy castle intended to sit on the summit of a dramatic snowy mountain.

DISTANT VIEW:
This castle will frequently be viewed from 200-400 blocks away.
Prioritize silhouette, tower composition and large architectural forms before micro-detail.

MAIN COMPOSITION:
Create:
- one dominant central palace
- one enormous central tower
- 6-10 secondary towers
- narrow gothic spires
- bridges between towers
- elevated courtyards
- flying buttresses
- large gothic windows
- enormous entrance gate
- several terraces
- defensive walls

CENTRAL TOWER:
Height approximately 120-130 blocks above the base.
The central tower must clearly dominate the skyline.

SECONDARY TOWERS:
Vary heights between approximately 55 and 100 blocks.
Avoid placing every tower symmetrically.
Create a balanced but organic skyline.

ARCHITECTURE:
Tall narrow proportions.
Deep gothic arches.
Strong vertical lines.
Flying buttresses.
Layered rooflines.
Elegant but intimidating appearance.

MATERIALS:
stone_bricks
cracked_stone_bricks
deepslate_bricks
deepslate_tiles
polished_deepslate
calcite
andesite
dark_oak
spruce

LIGHTING:
Many warm illuminated windows.
The castle should look inhabited and glow strongly when viewed at night.

SNOW:
Add snow layers to roofs, balconies and selected ledges.
Use packed_ice and blue_ice only sparingly.

FUNCTIONALITY:
Provide stairs between major floors.
Connect towers through bridges and corridors where practical.

STRUCTURAL REALISM:
No floating towers.
All bridges and extensions must have convincing support.

TERRAIN:
Only create the summit foundation immediately beneath the castle.
Do not generate the mountain itself.

OUTPUT:
Standalone WorldEdit-ready schematic intended for placement on separately created terrain.
