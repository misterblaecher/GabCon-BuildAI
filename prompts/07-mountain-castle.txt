PROJECT: Snowy Gothic Mountain Castle

TARGET:
approximately 130 blocks wide
95 blocks deep
135 blocks tall

ROLE:
This is the distant high-altitude landmark at the back of the valley.

Create an enormous gothic fantasy castle intended to sit on a separately built snowy mountain summit.

DISTANT VIEW:
The castle will frequently be viewed from 200-400 blocks away.
Prioritize silhouette, tower composition and large architectural forms before micro-detail.

MAIN COMPOSITION:
Create:
- one dominant central palace
- one enormous central tower
- 6-10 secondary towers
- narrow gothic spires
- bridges between towers
- elevated courtyards
- flying buttresses
- large gothic windows
- enormous entrance gate
- several terraces
- defensive walls

CENTRAL TOWER:
Height approximately 120-130 blocks above the base.
It must clearly dominate the skyline.

SECONDARY TOWERS:
Vary heights between approximately 55 and 100 blocks.
Avoid perfect symmetry.
Create a balanced but organic skyline.

ARCHITECTURE:
Tall narrow proportions.
Deep gothic arches.
Strong vertical lines.
Flying buttresses.
Layered rooflines.
Elegant, mysterious and imposing appearance.

MATERIALS:
stone_bricks
cracked_stone_bricks
deepslate_bricks
deepslate_tiles
polished_deepslate
calcite
andesite
dark_oak
spruce

LIGHTING:
Many warm illuminated windows.
The castle should feel inhabited and glow strongly at night.

SNOW:
Add snow layers to roofs, balconies and selected ledges.
Use packed-ice / blue-ice-like accents only sparingly.

FUNCTIONALITY:
Show plausible stair / bridge circulation between major masses.
Connect towers through bridges and corridors where practical.

STRUCTURAL REALISM:
No floating towers.
All bridges and extensions need convincing supports or buttresses.

FOUNDATION:
Only create a compact snowy stone summit plinth immediately beneath the castle.
Do NOT generate the mountain itself.
Do NOT generate a huge snowy landscape.

REFERENCE IMAGE REQUIREMENTS:
Use an elevated three-quarter isometric viewpoint.
Show the entire castle from foundation to tallest spire.
Keep every major tower in frame.
Show enough of the front and one side to understand depth and tower placement.
Do not hide the lower palace behind a mountain.
Do not use fog to obscure the architecture.
Do not crop spires.
Use only a small snowy foundation and a minimal background.
Prioritize silhouette and Minecraft-readable geometry over cinematic scenery.

OUTPUT:
A standalone Minecraft schematic-reference image for the mountain castle module.
