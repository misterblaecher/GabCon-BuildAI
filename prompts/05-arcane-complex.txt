PROJECT: Arcane Industrial Portal Complex

TARGET:
approximately 105 blocks wide
78 blocks deep
115 blocks tall

Create a huge vertically stacked magical-industrial Minecraft complex containing several portal machines.

STYLE:
Medieval gothic architecture
Victorian steampunk machinery
arcane fantasy technology

Avoid modern science-fiction architecture.

MAIN COMPOSITION:
Build the complex over several stone terraces at different elevations.

Create three major portal machines:
1. cyan portal
2. deep blue portal
3. purple portal

Each portal must have a distinct surrounding structure.

PORTALS:
The portal machines should be visible from far across the valley.
Use layered lighting and glass rather than simple flat colored rectangles.

CYAN MATERIALS:
cyan_stained_glass
sea_lantern
prismarine
soul_lantern

BLUE MATERIALS:
light_blue_stained_glass
blue_stained_glass
prismarine
sea_lantern

PURPLE MATERIALS:
purple_stained_glass
amethyst_block
crying_obsidian
end_rod

Use glowing materials sparingly enough that the structure remains mostly stone and industrial.

ARCHITECTURE:
Create:
- laboratories
- gothic halls
- mechanical towers
- bridges
- elevated platforms
- crane structures
- pipes
- boilers
- chains
- maintenance catwalks
- waterfalls passing between terraces

MATERIALS:
deepslate_bricks
blackstone
stone_bricks
tuff_bricks
dark_oak
spruce
copper
oxidized_copper
iron_bars
chains

VERTICALITY:
Strong multi-level composition.
Some portals should sit significantly higher than others.
Use bridges and stairs to visually connect the levels.

LIGHTING:
Dark architecture with intense localized portal lighting.

STRUCTURAL REALISM:
No unsupported floating laboratories or platforms.

TERRAIN:
Create only terraced cliff foundations immediately underneath the complex.
Do not generate an entire mountain.

OUTPUT:
Standalone WorldEdit-ready schematic.
