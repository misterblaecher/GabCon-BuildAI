PROJECT: Arcane Industrial Portal Complex

TARGET:
approximately 105 blocks wide
78 blocks deep
115 blocks tall

ROLE:
This is the magical-industrial landmark on the right side of the valley.

Create a huge vertically stacked Minecraft complex containing three distinct arcane machines / portal systems.

STYLE:
- medieval gothic architecture
- Victorian steampunk machinery
- arcane fantasy technology

Avoid modern clean science-fiction architecture.

MAIN COMPOSITION:
Build the complex over several clearly separated stone terraces at different elevations.

Create three major portal machines:
1. cyan portal
2. deep blue portal
3. purple portal

Each portal must:
- have a different architectural frame
- sit at a different height
- remain clearly visible in the reference image
- connect into the same overall industrial complex

PORTALS:
Use layered lighting, glass-like energy fields and heavy mechanical frames.
Do not use simple flat colored rectangles.

CYAN VISUAL MATERIALS:
cyan stained glass
sea-lantern-like light
prismarine-like accents
cool soul-blue light

BLUE VISUAL MATERIALS:
light blue / blue glass
prismarine-like details
cold bright light

PURPLE VISUAL MATERIALS:
purple glass
amethyst
crying-obsidian-like accents
small end-rod-like highlights

Use glowing materials sparingly so the architecture remains mostly stone and machinery.

ARCHITECTURE:
Create:
- laboratories
- gothic halls
- mechanical towers
- bridges
- elevated platforms
- crane structures
- pipes
- boilers
- chains
- maintenance catwalks
- controlled waterfalls between terraces

SERVER / CREATE VISUAL LANGUAGE:
Use machinery inspired by the GabCon server:
- andesite casings
- brass casings
- copper casings
- fluid tanks
- valves
- shafts
- gantry / crane-like assemblies
- mechanical frames

Do not require exact block IDs in the image.
The blueprint will select validated registry blocks later.

MATERIALS:
deepslate_bricks
blackstone
stone_bricks
tuff_bricks
dark_oak
spruce
copper
oxidized_copper
iron_bars
chains

VERTICALITY:
Strong multi-level composition.
Portal 1, 2 and 3 should sit on clearly different terraces.
Use bridges and stairs to visually connect levels.

LIGHTING:
Dark architecture with intense localized cyan / blue / purple portal lighting.
Retain warm amber lighting in occupied workshops.

STRUCTURAL REALISM:
No unsupported floating laboratories, portals or platforms.
Every major machine should have a visible structural frame.

FOUNDATION:
Create only terraced cliff foundations immediately underneath the complex.
Do not generate an entire mountain.

REFERENCE IMAGE REQUIREMENTS:
Use an elevated three-quarter view from the front / lake-facing side.
All three portal machines must be visible in the same image.
Do not hide one portal behind another.
Show the vertical terrace hierarchy clearly.
Show major bridges, stairs, cranes and laboratories.
Keep the whole complex in frame.
Minimal terrain and background.
Avoid fog and extreme glow.
Prioritize readable block geometry.

OUTPUT:
A standalone Minecraft schematic-reference image for the arcane complex module.
