PROJECT: Central Lakeside Arcane Gateway

TARGET:
approximately 48 blocks wide
32 blocks deep
55 blocks tall

Create the main magical landmark of the entire valley.
The structure stands at the end of a large ceremonial plaza beside the lake.

MAIN PORTAL:
Create one enormous vertical gothic portal approximately 22 blocks wide and 38 blocks tall.
The portal opening should be an elongated gothic arch rather than a simple rectangle.
Create several nested stone frames around the portal to produce strong depth.

Use:
deepslate_bricks
polished_deepslate
stone_bricks
blackstone
oxidized_copper

MAGICAL CORE:
Create a visually layered cyan magical interior using restrained combinations of:
cyan_stained_glass
light_blue_stained_glass
cyan_stained_glass_pane
sea_lantern
prismarine
dark_prismarine
soul_lantern

The portal must not be a flat colored wall.
Create at least three depth layers:
- outer magical frame
- inner glowing frame
- central energy opening

PORTAL CRYSTALS:
Place several crystal-like formations above and beside the portal.
Use amethyst_block, budding_amethyst, purple_stained_glass and end_rod.
Purple should accent the cyan portal rather than dominate it.

ARCHITECTURE:
Create:
- monumental staircase
- two secondary portal pylons
- gothic buttresses
- stone arches
- balconies
- copper mechanical details
- chains
- narrow towers
- lantern posts

PLAZA:
Create a multi-level plaza approximately 45x30 blocks.
Include broad stairs, retaining walls, planters, lantern posts,
bench-like stairs/slabs and several market locations.
The plaza should descend naturally toward the waterfront.

MARKET DETAILS:
Add small wooden market stalls around the edges.
Use colorful wool or carpet roofs sparingly.
Keep the portal itself unobstructed.

LIGHTING:
Strong cyan portal light contrasted with warm lantern lighting around the plaza.

Do not generate surrounding city buildings.
Do not generate the lake.
Do not generate large terrain.

OUTPUT:
Standalone WorldEdit-ready schematic.
