PROJECT: GabCon Steampunk Fantasy Valley

GLOBAL ART DIRECTION

This project recreates a gigantic Minecraft fantasy-steampunk valley surrounding a mountain lake.

The visual identity must stay consistent across every independent schematic.

STYLE:
- medieval European fantasy
- Victorian steampunk
- dark stone industrial architecture
- warm inhabited buildings
- copper machinery
- magical cyan and purple energy
- mountainous alpine environment
- dense vertical architecture
- highly detailed Minecraft build

GENERAL VISUAL LANGUAGE:
Architecture must feel handcrafted and old.
Avoid modern architecture.
Avoid clean science-fiction architecture.
Avoid giant flat rectangular walls.

Prefer:
- irregular footprints
- stepped building heights
- towers of different sizes
- overhanging roofs
- balconies
- arches
- buttresses
- exposed wooden beams
- bridges
- stairs
- terraces
- pipes
- gears
- water wheels
- cranes
- chimneys
- hanging lanterns
- chains

LIGHTING:
Warm amber lighting should dominate normal buildings.
Use lantern, soul_lantern and concealed glowstone when needed.

MAGICAL AREAS:
Use sea_lantern, cyan_stained_glass, light_blue_stained_glass,
blue_stained_glass, purple_stained_glass, amethyst_block,
budding_amethyst and crying_obsidian.

Normal city lighting = warm amber / orange.
Magic lighting = cyan / electric blue / purple.

Do not cover entire buildings in glowing blocks.
Magic lighting should remain localized.

MASSING:
Buildings should have strong silhouettes when viewed from 100-300 blocks away.
Large builds must have major primary masses, secondary towers and tertiary details.
Do not make every detail the same scale.

ARCHITECTURAL DETAIL:
Use stairs, slabs, walls, fences, trapdoors and panes extensively.
Break flat surfaces with recessed windows, columns, balconies, trim,
roof overhangs, buttresses and controlled material variation.

WEATHERING:
Stone surfaces should use restrained variation, roughly:
70% base stone
20% cracked / alternate stone
10% mossy or aged material

STRUCTURAL RULES:
No floating structures.
Bridges must connect physically to supports.
Large platforms need pillars, brackets, suspension or visible beams.
Player routes should be believable.
Doors, stairs, bridges and major platforms should preferably be traversable.

WORLD COMPOSITION:
The lake and terrain will be built separately.
Individual schematics should avoid generating large amounts of terrain.
Build only enough stone foundation to make each structure self-contained.
Every module must be suitable for later placement with WorldEdit/FAWE.
