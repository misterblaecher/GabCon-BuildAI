PROJECT: Central Waterfront Market District

TARGET:
approximately 160 blocks wide
110 blocks deep
55 blocks tall

Create a dense fantasy-steampunk lakeside town surrounding the main city plaza.

BUILDING COUNT:
Create approximately 14-18 major buildings.
Do not make them identical.

BUILDING TYPES:
Include:
- inn
- merchant houses
- artisan workshops
- warehouse
- tavern
- market hall
- harbor office
- residential townhouses
- small guild building
- boat workshop

STYLE:
Medieval timber-frame European architecture with subtle steampunk machinery.
Buildings should be visually rich but cozy rather than industrial.

HEIGHT:
Mostly 2-4 floors.
A few civic buildings may reach 5 floors.

STREETS:
Create irregular pedestrian streets.
Avoid a square grid.
Use stairs, small terraces, alleys, archways, bridges and retaining walls.

WATERFRONT:
One side of the district must open completely onto the lake.
Build stone quays, wooden piers, loading docks, a small crane,
boat mooring spaces, steps descending toward water and warehouse entrances.

CENTRAL MARKET:
Create an open marketplace with 10-14 small stalls.
Use restrained colorful awnings in cyan, blue, red and orange.

TREES AND VEGETATION:
Include several medium trees integrated into plazas.
Use leaves, flower boxes and vines to soften the stone architecture.

LIGHTING:
Extremely warm and inviting.
Use many hanging lanterns.
Make the district visually attractive at night.

ROOFS:
Use steep roofs and multiple roof heights.
Prefer dark_oak, spruce, deepslate_tiles and oxidized_copper accents.
Avoid large flat roofs.

DETAIL:
Use barrels, trapdoors, fences, chains, flower pots, stairs,
slabs, awnings, crates and small signs.

Do not generate the main portal.
Leave a broad plaza opening where the portal schematic can later be inserted.
Do not create the lake itself.

OUTPUT:
Standalone WorldEdit-ready city district schematic.
