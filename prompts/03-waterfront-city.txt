PROJECT: Central Waterfront Market District

TARGET:
approximately 220 blocks wide
160 blocks deep
55-65 blocks tall

ROLE:
This district wraps around the already-built central portal module.
It forms the inhabited heart of the valley.

Create a dense fantasy-steampunk lakeside town surrounding a deliberately EMPTY central ceremonial zone.

CRITICAL INTEGRATION REQUIREMENT:
The central portal is a separate schematic and must NOT be generated again.

Reserve a large unobstructed central insertion zone approximately:
95 blocks wide
80 blocks deep

This empty / open area must be visually obvious in the concept image.
It should connect naturally to surrounding streets and plazas.

Do not place buildings, trees, cranes or major stalls inside the reserved portal footprint.

The city should frame and visually lead toward the empty central landmark area.

BUILDING COUNT:
Create approximately 16-22 major buildings.
Do not make them identical.

BUILDING TYPES:
Include:
- inn
- merchant houses
- artisan workshops
- warehouse
- tavern
- market hall
- harbor office
- residential townhouses
- guild building
- boat workshop
- small mechanical workshop
- several narrow waterfront houses

STYLE:
Medieval timber-frame European architecture with subtle Victorian steampunk machinery.
The district should feel cozy, inhabited and dense rather than heavily industrial.

HEIGHT:
Mostly 2-4 floors.
A few civic / guild buildings may reach 5 floors.
Keep sightlines toward the central insertion zone.

STREETS:
Create irregular pedestrian streets.
Avoid a square grid.

Use:
- stairs
- small terraces
- alleys
- archways
- retaining walls
- short bridges
- covered passages

All major routes should visually converge toward the central portal zone or waterfront.

WATERFRONT:
One complete edge of the district opens toward the lake.

Build:
- stone quays
- wooden piers
- loading docks
- one or two small cranes
- boat mooring spaces
- stairs descending toward water
- warehouse doors facing the harbor

Do not generate the lake itself.
Leave a clean waterfront edge so the schematic can meet separately built water.

MARKETS:
Create several small market clusters, but keep them away from the reserved portal insertion zone.
Use restrained colorful awnings:
cyan
blue
red
orange

VEGETATION:
Use several medium trees, flower boxes and vines.
Keep vegetation architectural and controlled.
Do not hide buildings.

LIGHTING:
Warm, inviting nighttime lighting.
Many lanterns and illuminated windows.
Do not use excessive magical cyan except where subtle machinery accents are needed.

ROOFS:
Use steep roofs with varied heights.
Prefer:
dark_oak
spruce
deepslate_tiles
oxidized_copper accents

Avoid broad flat roofs.

DETAIL:
Use visually readable Minecraft details:
barrels
trapdoors
fences
chains
flower pots
stairs
slabs
awnings
crates
signs
small industrial fittings

SERVER / CREATE VISUAL LANGUAGE:
Small workshops may use subtle:
- andesite-like casings
- brass-like machinery
- copper-like machinery
- mechanical frames
- small pipe assemblies

The district must remain residential / commercial, not become another factory.

REFERENCE IMAGE REQUIREMENTS:
Use a high elevated three-quarter / near-isometric viewpoint.
The whole district must fit in frame.
The central empty portal insertion zone must be clearly visible and measurable by eye.
Show the waterfront edge, docks, streets and surrounding building ring simultaneously.
Do not place the camera at street level.
Do not fill the reserved center with decorative architecture.
Do not generate another portal.
Use minimal terrain and minimal lake water.
Prioritize layout readability over cinematic atmosphere.

OUTPUT:
A standalone Minecraft schematic-reference image for the city module, designed to wrap around module 01.
