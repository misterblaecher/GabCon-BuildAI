PROJECT: Gothic Industrial Railway Viaduct

TARGET:
approximately 125 blocks long
11 blocks wide
38 blocks tall

Create a monumental Minecraft stone railway viaduct designed to cross a deep fantasy mountain valley.

DESIGN:
Create a repeating series of enormous gothic arches.
The middle section should use a repeating pattern so it can later be duplicated with WorldEdit.

ARCHES:
Approximately 17-20 blocks wide.
Use tall narrow arch shapes rather than simple rectangular gaps.

SUPPORTS:
Create:
- massive stone pillars
- buttresses
- reinforcement arches
- maintenance platforms
- occasional iron supports

TOP DECK:
Create a flat railway deck.
Allow enough space for rails, a decorative custom train and a maintenance walkway.
Add protective walls or railings.

LIGHTING:
Place sparse industrial lanterns along the upper deck.

MATERIALS:
stone_bricks
cracked_stone_bricks
andesite
tuff_bricks
cobblestone
deepslate details
iron_bars
chains

STYLE:
Ancient monumental engineering combined with Victorian railway architecture.

STRUCTURAL REALISM:
Pillars must align convincingly with arches.
Avoid unnecessary floating decorations.

IMPORTANT:
The middle arch module should be visually repeatable.

TERRAIN:
Do not generate cliffs or valley terrain.

OUTPUT:
Standalone extendable railway viaduct schematic.
