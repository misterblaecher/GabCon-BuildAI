PROJECT: Gothic Industrial Railway Viaduct

TARGET:
approximately 125 blocks long
11-13 blocks wide
38-42 blocks tall

ROLE:
This viaduct crosses the rear portion of the valley and must be easy to extend or repeat.

Create a monumental Minecraft stone railway viaduct using a repeatable gothic arch system.

DESIGN:
Create a repeating series of enormous gothic arches.
The middle section must use a clearly repeatable structural module so it can later be extended with WorldEdit / mcbuild.

ARCHES:
Approximately 17-20 blocks wide.
Use tall pointed / gothic arch shapes rather than rectangular gaps.

Create enough repeated spans that the rhythm is obvious.

SUPPORTS:
Create:
- massive stone pillars
- buttresses
- reinforcement arches
- maintenance platforms
- occasional iron / copper reinforcement
- readable foundations at pillar bottoms

TOP DECK:
Create a flat railway deck with enough width for:
- rail line
- decorative custom train
- narrow maintenance walkway

Add protective walls / railings.

INDUSTRIAL DETAIL:
Use sparse Victorian railway details:
- lanterns
- iron braces
- copper / brass fittings
- small service platforms
- occasional Create-style mechanical components

Keep these secondary to the stone structure.

MATERIALS:
stone_bricks
cracked_stone_bricks
andesite
tuff_bricks
cobblestone
deepslate details
iron_bars
chains

STYLE:
Ancient monumental engineering combined with Victorian railway architecture.

STRUCTURAL REALISM:
Pillars must align convincingly with arches.
No floating deck sections.
Do not over-decorate the repeating center spans.

TERRAIN:
Do not generate cliffs, valley walls or mountains.
Use only tiny support pads under pillars if necessary.

REFERENCE IMAGE REQUIREMENTS:
Use a long near-side elevation / shallow three-quarter viewpoint.
The ENTIRE viaduct length must fit inside the frame from one end to the other.
The camera should be high enough to reveal the railway deck but low enough to clearly read every arch.
Do not use strong foreshortening.
Do not hide arches behind terrain.
Do not place a large train that obscures the deck.
Make the repeatable center arch module extremely clear.
Use a neutral minimal background.

OUTPUT:
A standalone Minecraft schematic-reference image for an extendable railway viaduct module.
