PROJECT: Steampunk Mountain Observatory

TARGET:
approximately 68 blocks wide
58 blocks deep
92 blocks tall

Create a dramatic fantasy-steampunk astronomical observatory suitable for placement on a high mountain overlooking a valley.

MAIN LANDMARK:
Create one tall cylindrical observatory tower terminating in a large observation dome.
Attach an enormous telescope to the upper observatory.
The telescope must be clearly recognizable from a distance.

TELESCOPE:
Length approximately 35-45 blocks.
Point it diagonally upward toward the sky.

Create:
- large cylindrical telescope body
- wide front lens
- copper rings
- mechanical support arms
- giant rotating mount
- counterweight
- gear/wheel-like decorative structures

The telescope must appear structurally supported.

ARCHITECTURE:
Combine medieval stone architecture with Victorian scientific machinery.

Create:
- central tower
- observation dome
- lower laboratory buildings
- external staircases
- balconies
- stone arches
- narrow bridges
- machinery platforms
- antennas or lightning rods
- several smaller towers

LIGHTING:
Mix warm orange lantern lighting with subtle blue scientific lighting.

SILHOUETTE:
Strong vertical profile.
The telescope and dome must remain visually identifiable from at least 150 blocks away.

TERRAIN:
Only create a rough stone cliff foundation approximately 8-12 blocks high.
Do not generate a full mountain.
Do not generate trees or surrounding landscape.

OUTPUT:
Standalone WorldEdit-ready schematic.
