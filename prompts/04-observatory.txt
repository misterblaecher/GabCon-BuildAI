PROJECT: Steampunk Mountain Observatory

TARGET:
approximately 68 blocks wide
58 blocks deep
92 blocks tall

ROLE:
This is a high-elevation scientific landmark on the right / rear side of the valley.

Create a dramatic fantasy-steampunk astronomical observatory suitable for placement on a high mountain.

MAIN LANDMARK:
Create one tall cylindrical observatory tower terminating in a large observation dome.
Attach an enormous telescope to the upper observatory.

The telescope must be unmistakable from a distance.

TELESCOPE:
Length approximately 35-45 blocks.
Point it diagonally upward toward the sky at approximately 25-40 degrees.

Create:
- large cylindrical telescope body
- wide front lens
- copper rings
- mechanical support arms
- giant rotating mount
- counterweight
- gear / wheel-like structures
- maintenance access around the mount

The telescope must look physically supported and buildable in Minecraft.

ARCHITECTURE:
Combine medieval stone architecture with Victorian scientific machinery.

Create:
- central tower
- observation dome
- lower laboratory buildings
- external staircases
- balconies
- stone arches
- narrow bridges
- machinery platforms
- antennas / lightning rods
- 2-4 smaller towers

MATERIALS:
stone_bricks
deepslate_bricks
polished_deepslate
tuff_bricks
dark_oak
spruce
copper
oxidized_copper
iron_bars
chains
glass
light_blue_stained_glass

SERVER / CREATE VISUAL LANGUAGE:
Use mechanical details suggesting:
- brass casings
- copper casings
- andesite machinery
- rotating mounts
- shafts
- valves
- mechanical braces

LIGHTING:
Warm orange lantern lighting in occupied rooms.
Subtle cool blue scientific lighting around the telescope / dome.

SILHOUETTE:
Strong vertical profile.
The dome and telescope must remain recognizable from at least 150 blocks away.

FOUNDATION:
Only create a compact rough stone summit / cliff foundation approximately 8-12 blocks high.
Do not generate a full mountain.
Do not generate forests or surrounding landscape.

REFERENCE IMAGE REQUIREMENTS:
Use a three-quarter view from slightly above and from the side of the telescope.
Show:
- the entire tower
- the complete telescope barrel
- the lens
- the support mount
- the lower laboratories
- bridges / stairs
- the full foundation

Do not let the telescope point directly toward or away from the camera.
Its full length must be visible in profile / three-quarter.
Do not crop the telescope tip or tower roof.
Minimal background.
Prioritize mechanical readability and Minecraft geometry.

OUTPUT:
A standalone Minecraft schematic-reference image for the observatory module.
