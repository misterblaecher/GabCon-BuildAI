PROJECT: Great Cliffside Waterwheel Factory

TARGET:
approximately 145 blocks wide
85 blocks deep
125 blocks tall

Create a gigantic fantasy-steampunk factory climbing vertically up a cliff.
This is not one building; it is an interconnected industrial district.

MASSING:
Create at least 8 clearly identifiable architectural masses.
They should climb upward from the waterfront toward the cliff summit.
The skyline must be strongly asymmetrical.

WATER WHEELS:
Create 4 giant water wheels with approximate diameters:
- 29 blocks
- 25 blocks
- 21 blocks
- 15 blocks

The two largest wheels must dominate the front facade.
Build convincing circular wheels using dark oak, spruce, stairs, slabs,
fences and trapdoors.
Add central axles and structural supports.
Create water channels and waterfalls feeding several wheels.

VERTICAL FACTORY:
Create:
- enormous lower industrial halls
- mid-level workshops
- upper machinery towers
- observation towers
- narrow industrial spires

Connect everything through bridges, external staircases, catwalks,
conveyor-like structures, elevated rails and maintenance platforms.

SMOKESTACKS:
Create 7-10 chimneys.
They should vary strongly in height, diameter and shape.
Some should be brick, some dark stone, some with copper bands.

CRANES:
Create at least 3 large mechanical cranes.
One should extend above the waterfront.
Use logs, fences, chains, copper and iron_bars.

WORKSHOP AREAS:
Include visible loading bays with crates, barrels, work tables,
furnaces, rails, chains and industrial lighting.

ARCHITECTURE:
Blend medieval stone architecture with Victorian industrial engineering.
Do not make it look like a modern factory.

WINDOWS:
Use large warm illuminated industrial windows framed with dark structural beams.

WATER:
Create several vertical waterfalls passing directly between buildings.
The interaction of water and machinery is a major visual feature.

LIGHTING:
Use many small points of warm lantern light.
Use cyan magical machinery only in a few locations.
At least one vertical cyan energy machine may be visible.

FOUNDATION:
Build only the immediate cliff structure required to support the factory.
Do not create the surrounding mountains or lake.

OUTPUT:
Standalone WorldEdit-ready industrial schematic.
