PROJECT: Great Cliffside Waterwheel Factory

TARGET:
approximately 145 blocks wide
85 blocks deep
125 blocks tall

ROLE:
This is the huge industrial landmark on the left side of the final valley composition.

Create a gigantic fantasy-steampunk factory climbing vertically up a limited rocky foundation.
This is not one building; it is an interconnected industrial district.

MASSING:
Create at least 8 clearly identifiable architectural masses.
They should climb upward from the waterfront level toward the rear / upper cliff foundation.
The skyline must be strongly asymmetrical.

WATER WHEELS:
Create 4 giant water wheels with approximate diameters:
- 29 blocks
- 25 blocks
- 21 blocks
- 15 blocks

The two largest wheels must dominate the visible front / lake-facing facade.
Make their axles, spokes and supports clearly readable.

Use a Minecraft-readable construction language:
dark oak
spruce
stairs
slabs
fences
trapdoors
copper / brass mechanical hubs

Create channels and short waterfalls feeding several wheels.

VERTICAL FACTORY:
Create:
- enormous lower industrial halls
- mid-level workshops
- upper machinery towers
- observation towers
- narrow industrial spires
- loading bays
- boiler buildings

Connect them with:
- bridges
- external staircases
- catwalks
- elevated rails
- maintenance platforms
- mechanical galleries

SMOKESTACKS:
Create 7-10 chimneys.
Vary height, diameter and shape.
Use a mix of:
- brick
- dark stone
- copper / brass banding

CRANES:
Create at least 3 large mechanical cranes.
One should project above the waterfront edge.
Their booms and supports must be fully visible.

SERVER / CREATE VISUAL LANGUAGE:
Use visible machinery inspired by the GabCon server palette:
- andesite-like casings
- brass-like casings
- copper-like casings
- shafts / mechanical joints
- industrial frames
- fluid-tank-like forms
- valves and pipe networks

Do not turn the build into modern technology.
It must remain Victorian / medieval fantasy.

WORKSHOP AREAS:
Include visible loading areas with:
- crates
- barrels
- furnaces
- workshop tables
- rails
- chains
- industrial lights

WINDOWS:
Use large warm illuminated industrial windows framed with dark structural beams.

WATER:
Water interacting with machinery is a major visual feature.
Create several controlled waterfalls / channels passing between buildings.
Do not let waterfalls hide major architectural forms.

LIGHTING:
Many small warm amber lights.
Use cyan magical machinery only in a few focal points.
One vertical cyan energy device may be present.

FOUNDATION:
Show only enough rocky foundation to explain the vertical levels.
Do not generate the full mountain.
Do not generate the surrounding lake or city.

REFERENCE IMAGE REQUIREMENTS:
Use an elevated three-quarter view from the lake-facing side.
The entire factory must fit inside the frame.
All 4 water wheels should be visible or clearly inferable.
At least 6 chimneys should be visible.
Show the highest tower, waterfront crane, bridges and multiple vertical levels simultaneously.
Do not let rock formations hide the lower factory.
Use minimal background.
Avoid atmospheric fog covering the machinery.
Prioritize block-readable Minecraft geometry.

OUTPUT:
A standalone Minecraft schematic-reference image for the cliffside factory module.
